Clinical Waiting Coordination Capabilities

Engineered for high-volume outpatient clinics and primary care centers.

🏷️

Minimal Patient Token

Discreet alphanumeric tokens (e.g. GM-003) preserve patient privacy on public displays while eliminating verbal confusion and line disputes.

⏳

Wait Time with Uncertainty

Stochastically computed waiting windows (e.g. 15-25m) based on real-time consultation duration averages and active desk counts, setting transparent patient expectations.

🚨

Configurable Priority Triage

Support for Emergency, Urgent, and Normal clinical tiers. High-priority patients automatically bubble to the head of the queue with documented triage justification.

📝

Mandatory Manual Override Reasons

Strict clinical governance: staff cannot skip, cancel, or re-order patients without logging a mandatory operational override justification.

📢

Reception Queue Display Board

High-contrast, large-format screen optimized for lobby TV monitors with real-time calling cards, counter direction, and audible chimes.

📊

Daily Operations Analytics

Automated end-of-day operational summaries computing tokens served, average patient wait times, consultation times, and peak hourly traffic.
